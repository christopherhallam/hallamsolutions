About ElasticRender

Deploy the visualisation, not a permanently idle render farm.

ElasticRender makes large, visually rich 3D datasets available through an ordinary web experience, with rendering capacity created when people are actively exploring.

On-demand capacity

Rendering resources respond to demand, making interactive applications practical when usage is intermittent, bursty or difficult to predict.

Large assets stay close to compute

Complex collections can remain server-side instead of requiring every visitor to download and process the complete dataset locally.

Thin-client experience

The browser handles interaction and presentation while rendered views are produced remotely, reducing dependence on the visitor’s graphics hardware.

Technology

Interactive access to demanding 3D collections.

ElasticRender supports curated scenes and individual objects, responsive frames while a view is moving, and higher-quality frames when someone stops to inspect a result. It can complement provisioned rendering for sustained workloads while absorbing occasional demand without a permanently running renderer.

The approach is suited to museums and cultural heritage, photogrammetry, surveying and geospatial visualisation, engineering and infrastructure, digital twins, architecture and remote inspection.
